Data Analyst

Job Description:

We are looking for a Data Analyst to analyze business data and generate actionable insights.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean and validate data from multiple sources.
- Perform data analysis and exploratory analysis.
- Create dashboards and reports for business stakeholders.
- Track and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Work with SQL databases to retrieve and transform data.
- Use Excel and Power BI for reporting and visualization.
- Identify trends, patterns and data quality issues.
- Communicate analytical findings to stakeholders.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science or a related field.
- Basic to intermediate SQL skills.
- Knowledge of Python for data analysis.
- Experience with Power BI and Excel.
- Understanding of data cleaning and data visualization.
- Knowledge of statistics and analytical techniques.
- Good communication and problem-solving skills.
